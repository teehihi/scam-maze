# Code of Conduct

SCAMMAZE is an educational project about online scams. Many people who join the
conversation — players, testers, contributors — may have been scammed themselves.
We want this to be a place where nobody feels ashamed to ask, report or learn.

## Our pledge

We, as contributors and maintainers, commit to making participation in this
project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, especially with people sharing a scam they fell for.
- Give and accept constructive feedback; focus on the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Respect privacy: never post someone's personal data, screenshots with
  identifiers, or private conversations without consent.
- Credit others' work, including AI-assisted contributions (see
  [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)).

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, personal attacks.
- Victim-blaming ("only stupid people get scammed").
- Sharing **live** malicious links, real phishing kits, stolen credentials,
  or step-by-step instructions to run a scam against real people.
- Doxxing, or publishing anyone's private information.
- Sexualized content, or any content that harms minors.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests,
discussions, the live demo, and events where someone represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report incidents privately to the maintainers at
**tee21052005@gmail.com**. All reports are reviewed promptly and kept
confidential.

Maintainers may, depending on severity:

1. **Correct** — a private note explaining what was inappropriate.
2. **Warn** — a formal warning with consequences for continued behavior.
3. **Temporary ban** — no interaction with the project for a set period.
4. **Permanent ban** — for sustained or severe violations.

Malicious content (for example a live phishing URL) is removed immediately,
without waiting for a discussion.
